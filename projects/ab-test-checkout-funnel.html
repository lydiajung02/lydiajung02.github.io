How Did I Do This?

  1. 1. Experiment design Before launch, I ran a power analysis: to detect a 3pt lift on a ~18% baseline conversion rate at 80% power and α = 0.05, I needed roughly 10,700 visitors per arm — I set a target of 11,400 to leave margin.
  2. 2. Randomization & collection Visitors were bucketed at first checkout visit via a hashed user ID to keep them in the same arm for the full session, and I let the test run a full two weeks to cover weekday/weekend purchasing patterns rather than stopping early on a promising interim result.
  3. 3. Statistical test Used a two-proportion z-test rather than a t-test since the outcome is binary (converted / did not). Checked the standard np > 5 / n(1-p) > 5 assumption for the normal approximation before trusting the p-value — both arms cleared it comfortably.
  4. 4. Result interpretation The variant converted at 21.9% vs. 17.7% for control — a 4.2pt lift, p < 0.0001. I also checked the 95% confidence interval on the lift (3.2pt to 5.2pt) rather than reporting the point estimate alone, since the interval is what actually informs the rollout risk.
  5. 5. Recommendation Shipped the variant to 100% of traffic. Flagged for the team that the lift was measured against a specific two-week window and should be re-validated after a full quarter in case of seasonality.

Sales Forecasting with Time Series

How Did I Do This?

  1. 1. Data prep Aggregated daily transactions to monthly totals, aligned to calendar months, and filled two short gaps (a system outage month) using the prior year's same-month value adjusted for the year-over-year growth trend rather than a flat interpolation.
  2. 2. Decomposition Ran a seasonal decomposition first to confirm what I was dealing with: a clear upward trend plus a strong December seasonal spike (~1.6x an average month). This ruled out a naive linear-trend model on its own.
  3. 3. Model selection Compared a seasonal ARIMA (SARIMA, tuned via grid search on (p,d,q)(P,D,Q)) against Facebook Prophet, which handles holiday effects more directly. Prophet edged out SARIMA on holdout error and needed far less manual tuning, so it became the production model.
  4. 4. Backtesting Used walk-forward validation — train on months 1–30, predict month 31, then roll forward — rather than a single train/test split, since a single split can get lucky or unlucky on which months land in the test set. Averaged MAPE across folds: 6.3%.
  5. 5. Handoff Delivered the forecast as a monthly-refreshed CSV plus this chart, with the confidence band included deliberately so the inventory team would size orders around a range, not a single point estimate.

LWVO Member Demographics Analysis

A consulting project for the League of Women Voters of Oakland (LWVO): a member survey plus a ZIP-code choropleth map to find underrepresented demographics and turn that into a concrete outreach strategy.

How Did I Do This?

  1. 1. Understand the ask LWVO wanted to diversify their member base but had no structured view of who their current members actually were, or where the gaps sat geographically — so the first job was building that baseline, not jumping straight to recommendations.
  2. 2. Survey design & collection Designed a survey to collect demographic data from active members — not just who they are, but why they joined, how they first heard about LWVO, and which causes they wanted the organization to prioritize — so the results could tie engagement patterns back to specific, actionable levers.
  3. 3. Data cleaning Manually cleaned survey responses where free-text answers didn't match the expected format (e.g. some respondents entered their actual age instead of birth year), since this kind of inconsistency silently corrupts any age-based analysis downstream if left unhandled.
  4. 4. Choropleth mapping Grouped membership data by ZIP code and built a Folium choropleth map with per-ZIP markers showing racial composition, household income, and age distribution — chosen specifically because a map communicates "where the gaps are" to non-technical LWVO leadership far faster than a table or dashboard would.
  5. 5. Individual deep-dives Each team member took one relationship between member identity and engagement (e.g. NPS score by engagement frequency, education level by age group, how members first heard about LWVO by demographic) and analyzed it with Python's stats and visualization libraries, so the final poster synthesized seven independent angles rather than one surface-level pass.
  6. 6. Recommendations Translated the findings into three concrete actions: revamp or retire underperforming social media platforms, strengthen marketing in lower-income ZIPs, and set explicit diversity targets for the next vision plan — the map made clear exactly which ZIPs (94603, 94609, 94613, 94621) had fewer than 10 members and were the highest-leverage outreach targets.
